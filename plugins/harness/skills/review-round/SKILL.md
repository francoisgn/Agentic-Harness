# Review round of PR $ARGUMENTS

Follow `.harness/docs/REVIEW-ROUNDS.md` (otherwise `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/docs/REVIEW-ROUNDS.md`):

1. **Read everything**: inline comments, "Outside diff range", nitpicks, "Prompt for AI agents", **each** open
   thread, the state of the checks. Reread the branch head (SHA): a finding on an old head may already be settled.
2. For each finding, **trace a real path** (input → failure). Classify it: real → fix; unfounded or
   disproportionate → reasoned reply; **security → stays open until the maintainer decides**.
3. **Fix the class**: other entry points, same pattern elsewhere, reverse order, other limits. One test per case,
   seen failing without the fix.
4. **Counter-check**: a separate subagent receives the findings, the diff and `LESSONS.md` (not your conclusions)
   and looks for what the fix misses or breaks. Integrate.
5. **One commit, one push** for the whole round. Optional nitpicks do not trigger a push on their own.
6. **One reply per thread** (fixing commit or reason), then resolve the thread; findings outside the diff: one PR
   comment. Every reply ends with the footer `---` / `_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_`.
7. Check that the bot reviewed **the new head**; otherwise ask the operator to post `@coderabbitai review`
   (mentions posted from the cloud are altered).
8. A check that fails within seconds with no log = infrastructure, not "CI red"; one rerun at most.
9. **Lessons**: each finding already covered by `LESSONS.md` = a self-review step to tighten; a new kind = propose a
   row (to the project, or to the harness by PR if it applies elsewhere).
