# Review round of PR $ARGUMENTS

Follow `.harness/docs/REVIEW-ROUNDS.md` (otherwise `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/docs/REVIEW-ROUNDS.md`):

0. **Two reviews, not one**: the review service on the PR reviews the **code** (diff only); the adversarial review
   (intent, done before "Ready") is separate. A round handles the former; do not blend them.
1. **Read everything**: inline comments, "Outside diff range", nitpicks, "Prompt for AI agents", **each** open
   thread, the state of the checks. Reread the branch head (SHA): a finding on an old head may already be settled.
2. For each finding, **trace a real path** (input → failure). Classify it: real → fix; unfounded or
   disproportionate → reasoned reply; **security → stays open until the maintainer decides**.
3. **Fix the class**: other entry points, same pattern elsewhere, reverse order, other limits. One test per case,
   seen failing without the fix.
4. **Counter-check**: a separate subagent, isolated from your context, receives the findings, the diff and `LESSONS.md` (not your conclusions)
   and looks for what the fix misses or breaks. Integrate.
5. **One commit, one push** for the whole round (the PR is ready: never a push "to see whether CI passes"). Optional
   nitpicks do not trigger a push on their own.
6. **One reply per thread** (fixing commit or reason), then resolve the thread; findings outside the diff: one PR
   comment. Every reply ends with the footer `---` / `_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_`.
7. Check that the bot reviewed **the new head**; otherwise ask the operator to post `@coderabbitai review`
   (mentions posted from the cloud are altered). If the service skips the repository (skip notice) or never reviews
   the head, fall back as REVIEW-ROUNDS §The two reviews says (`review` job, else a fresh sub-agent of another model
   on the diff only) and say in the PR which one was used.
8. **Convergence**: a round with no finding on the current head ends the loop (one clean round, within 3 cycles).
   List the settled findings in the PR with their reason, each line starting with `[fixed]`,
   `[declined: false positive]` or `[declined: not worth it]`; a reopened one is answered by pointing to that record
   and counts as new only with new evidence. A security finding stays open until the maintainer decides.
   Round done: `.harness/scripts/merge-gate.py <PR>` lists what still blocks a merge (stale approval, open thread…).
9. A check that fails within seconds with no log = infrastructure, not "CI red"; one rerun at most.
10. **Lessons**: each finding already covered by `LESSONS.md` = a self-review step to tighten; a new kind = propose a
   row (to the project, or to the harness by PR if it applies elsewhere).
